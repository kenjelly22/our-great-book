# The Crows

Once upon a time some crows perched outside my window. I named them and fed them bread. They returned the next day and I called their names and fed them more bread. This continued for several days. Eventually, the crows began to bring me shiny trinkets in exchange for the bread. I began to train them. Soon I had them flying in formation and raining down poop upon my enemies. My neighbors Frank and Clara were confused and afraid. I laughed as I called my crows to once again do my bidding.
